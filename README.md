# Dessa AI notes

Plain-language notes on **Dessa**, Block's speech-to-speech research model, with links to the independent [Dessa AI Guide](https://dessaai.org/).

> Not affiliated with Block. Statements about the model are attributed to the publisher unless stated otherwise.

## What Block says
- Learned from human conversation audio and responds with speech.
- Trained from scratch **without text or pretrained models** (central research claim).
- **Full duplex**: can listen and speak at the same time.

These are publisher statements, not an independent inspection of the unreleased model.

## Terms
| Term | Answers | Does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Speech recognition | How spoken words are transcribed | Anything about speech output |
| Text-to-speech | How text becomes audio | How the reply was composed |
| Speech-to-speech | Audio in, audio out | The internal architecture |
| Full duplex | When the system can listen/speak | Conversation quality in every setting |

## Questions for any voice-AI demo
1. What did the model learn from?
2. What goes in and what comes out?
3. When can it listen and when can it speak?
4. Is there a paper, benchmark, API or download?
